# Pre-registration: E21, does the audit change a decision on natural data? (2026-10-24)

This document fixes the design before any run. The code is in `exp/e21_natural_audit.py`, and the results go to `results/icde27/e21_natural_audit.json`.

**Motivation.** MODIFY.md (§1, Reviewer D, P1) notes that no natural-data case is reported in which the cross-cluster audit (condition (ii)) changes a decision. Its preventing the mean from being underestimated is not the same thing as changing a decision.

## Design
- **Data:** natural aliases only, with no injected probes or conflicts.
  - **GPG 2017–2025:** the same rows as E17. The left-hand side is the employer name, with the company number as oracle.
  - **NDC:** the left-hand side is the labeler name, with the labeler-code graph as oracle.
- **Right-hand sides (fixed now):** every other per-row attribute below, compared by Layer-1 equality.
  - GPG: empid, postcode, sic, size, year, Address, ResponsiblePerson, CurrentName, CompanyLinkToGPGInfo.
  - NDC: labcode, appno, brand, generic, mktcat, ptype, dosage form, route, substance.
- **Parameters:** δ = 0.05, θ = 0.9, hybrid proxy, k = 5. α = 0.05 is split over the candidates of each dataset.
- **Exact quantities per candidate, computed from the truth:**
  - g3 under the oracle;
  - g3 on the raw strings;
  - μ_i, the mean of the no-audit estimator (condition (i) only);
  - μ = μ_i + μ_ii, the mean of Algorithm 1 with a complete inverse.
- **Flags:**
  - *audit-decisive:* μ_i < δ ≤ μ;
  - *raw hides a violation:* g3_raw ≤ δ < g3_oracle.
- **Runs for flagged candidates:** both certifiers run for 3 seeds. One is Algorithm 1 with a complete inverse; the other is the no-audit estimator with the same proxy and no inverse. Each is scored against g3_oracle.

## What is reported
- the number of audit-decisive candidates, with their g3, μ_i and μ;
- the decisions of both certifiers on every flagged candidate;
- the number of candidates whose raw strings hide a violation.

If there are none, the paper says that no natural candidate was found on which the audit changes a decision. The audit's value then rests on the injected two-source and hidden-alias probes. The candidate list and δ are not changed after the results are seen.
