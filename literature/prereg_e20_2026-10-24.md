# Pre-registration: E20, oracle cost with N, D and |C| varied separately (2026-10-24)

This document fixes the design before any run. The code is in `exp/e20_scaling.py`, and the results go to `results/icde27/e20_scaling.json`.

**Motivation.** MODIFY.md P1 (scalability) points out a confound in the existing E2 curve (5k to 640k rows): it grows the row count N and the domain size D together.

## Design
All three sweeps use GeoNames with a complete inverse, δ = 0.05, α = 0.05 split over the candidates, θ = 0.9 and 2 seeds.

| Sweep | Table | Varied | Held fixed | Candidates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **rows** | 16k cities (cities15000) | Every row repeated 1, 4 or 16 times, so N grows | Every domain and every g3 | The eight FDs of E2 |
| **dom** | 4k, 16k or 64k cities, all from cities500.txt | D | N = 60,000, by uniform resampling with replacement (g3 recomputed) | The eight FDs of E2 |
| **cands** | 16k cities | Size of a nested subset of the unary candidates (8, 16, 32 or all), in a fixed random order per seed | The table | The subset |

Row repetition is used only as a control for N; it is not presented as a realistic workload.

**Recorded per run:**
- calls, split into forward and inverse;
- samples;
- decisions against the exact g3;
- canonicalize-all cost;
- the maximum and mean alias-class size and the maximum neighbour-set size per tolerant attribute (which bound κ).

## Expectations stated in advance (Theorems 1–2)
- **rows:** samples and calls stay roughly constant as N grows. The proxy-build time does not grow either, because it depends on D.
- **dom:** calls grow with D through cache misses, at most by the factor 3 + 3κ per sample. Canonicalize-all grows linearly in D.
- **cands:** total calls grow sublinearly in |C|, because caches are shared. Per-candidate samples grow only logarithmically through α/|C|.

Every outcome is reported, including any deviation from these expectations. The result enters the paper as at most one sentence, together with a pointer to the artifact.
