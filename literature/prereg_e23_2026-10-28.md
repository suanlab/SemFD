# Pre-registration: E23, E20's N / D / |C| separation repeated on Tax (2026-10-28)

This document fixes the design before any run. The code goes in `exp/e23_scaling_tax.py`, and the result goes to `results/icde27/e23_scaling_tax.json`. The plan is `literature/improvement_plan_2026-10-28.md` (item E23); PAPER_AUDIT I19 notes that the scaling evidence is GeoNames only.

## Question
On a second dataset, do oracle calls stay flat in N, grow with the domain D, and grow sublinearly in the number of candidates |C|, as E20 found on GeoNames?

## Fixed design
- **Dataset:** Tax (`semfd.data.tax`, ρ = 0.2, with its injected near-miss probes), complete inverse, δ = 0.05, α/|C|, θ = 0.9, shared oracle caches within a run, seeds 0 and 1. The certify call, its random streams and the recorded fields are the same as E20 (`exp/e20_scaling.run`); the stream tag starts with `e23`.
- **rows:** the 25k-row Tax sample (`n_rows=25000`), every row repeated r = 1, 4, 16 times (N = 25k–400k; every domain and g3 fixed). The candidates are Tax's listed FDs (`ds.fds`).
- **dom:** Tax samples of 12.5k, 50k and 200k rows (`n_rows`), each resampled uniformly with replacement to N = 60,000 rows, so D grows while N is fixed. The candidates are `ds.fds`, and g3 is recomputed on the resampled table.
- **cands:** the 50k-row sample. The candidates are nested subsets of all its unary candidates (the same rule as E20: different base attributes, excluding pairs true by construction), in a fixed random order (`default_rng(200 + seed)`), of sizes 8, 16, 32 and all.

## Pre-stated expectations and criteria
- **rows:** calls flat within ±10% across r.
- **dom:** calls increase with D. Where a resampled probe lands near δ, calls may exceed canonicalize-all, as at 35k cities in E20; this is reported, not tuned away.
- **cands:** calls increase sublinearly in |C|; the ratio to canonicalize-all is reported.
- **Correctness:** no false certification. Misses are counted.
- **Whatever the outcome**, the result is reported. In the paper it may extend the E20 sentence by at most one clause, and the Threats phrase "scaling on GeoNames only" changes accordingly.

## Not changed by this experiment
System defaults, E20's numbers and Tables II, III, VI and VIII.
